How I direct a small team of AI agents to help me stay organised

I’m a History graduate who works in an outdoor shop in London, and I direct a small team of AI agents in a system called Vulcan. Each one is Claude, the AI made by Anthropic, given one job and a name; most chose their own. I use Cairn for general advice, Belay for work-related projects, Kochi for my volunteering and portfolio, Setter for the system itself, and Lark, which sorts my email into tasks before I start work for the day.

Every chat with an AI starts with no memory of the last one, so everything that matters goes into plain notes that the main agents read first and update last. I’m not a developer (yet). I set the rules; the agents write and test the code. The full write-up covers how I limited what an email can make them do.
